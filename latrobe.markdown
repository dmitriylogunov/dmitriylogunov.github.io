I started from the specifications document and built the UI for the Advanced Standing Pre-Offer (ASPO) system at La Trobe University to the production and business acceptance stage. The platform handles credit assessments for international students and reduced admissions response times while improving student satisfaction ratings. It automates much of the workflow and matches previous study records. The system integrates with StudyLink and the Student Information System, helping admissions staff process advanced standing applications while keeping audit trails and coordinating with academics.
